05 Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still wondering about something? Write to us on WhatsApp, we answer in minutes.

Which driving licence do I need?

For the 50 cc and the electric moped, any car licence (B) or an AM licence is enough. For the 125 cc you need a car licence held for at least three years, or an A1, A2 or A motorbike licence. Licences issued outside the EU are accepted together with an International Driving Permit.

Is there a minimum age?

18 for the 50 cc and electric models, 21 for the 125 cc. You will also need a valid ID or passport and a debit or credit card for the deposit.

What does the deposit cover?

The deposit, €150 to €200 depending on the model, is held on your card and released at drop-off. It covers small damage, a lost helmet or a missing lock. Add the damage waiver for €8 a day and the deposit is halved.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Every rental includes third-party liability insurance, as required by Spanish law. Roadside assistance within Valencia is included too, just call us.

Do you deliver?

Delivery and collection are free anywhere in Valencia city centre, every day from 9:00 to 20:00. Airport, Malvarrosa and Patacona deliveries are €15.

What about fuel and charging?

Petrol mopeds go out with a full tank and come back with a full tank. The nearest petrol station is 400 m from our base. The E-Volt is charged every morning and does around 70 km on one charge, enough for a full day in the city.

Can two people ride?

Yes. All our mopeds are two-seaters and come with two helmets at no extra cost.

What if it rains or my plans change?

Valencia has around 300 sunny days a year, but if the forecast turns you can move your booking to another day or cancel for free up to 24 hours before pick-up.
